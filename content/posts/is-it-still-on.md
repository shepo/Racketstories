It drizzled on Tuesday. Twenty minutes, at lunchtime. By six it was dry, the courts were fine, the sky was that nothing grey it goes in November. My adult group starts at seven.

Here is every way I was asked if it was still on.

![Coach at his desk](organiser-desk "Me at 5.40pm, answering the same question for the eleventh time.")

<!--more-->

1. "Is it still on tonight?" Yes.

2. "Is tonight still on?" Same question. Backwards. Yes.

3. "Just checking it's on?" You weren't checking anything. You were asking me. Yes.

4. "Are we still playing??" Two question marks. Yes.

5. "Looks a bit wet here, still on?" Where's here? You live eleven miles away. Yes.

6. "Saw it rained earlier, is it on or off?" It rained at lunchtime. It's half five. It is on.

7. A thumbs up emoji, followed by a question mark emoji. I don't know what that is. I'm assuming it's the same question. Yes.

8. A phone call. A PHONE CALL. To ask if it's on. Nobody's rung me since 2019. Yes.

9. "Sorry if this has already been asked but is it still on?" It has. Scroll up. Yes.

10. "Is it on? I can't do tonight anyway, just wondering." Then why do you need to know? Yes.

11. "Still on?" from someone who isn't in the group. Never been in the group. I don't know who this is. Yes, I suppose.

That's eleven. For eight people. Two of them asked twice.

And here's the thing. I had already said it was on. At four o'clock. In the group. "ON TONIGHT, COURTS FINE, SEE YOU AT 7." Capital letters. I don't use capital letters. I used capital letters. It's sitting there, top of the chat, and below it are eleven people asking if it's on.

Nobody reads the message. Everybody sends the message. Every coach I know has the same problem, and every one of them has tried the same fixes. Pinning it. Putting it in bold. Sending it again at six. Sending it at six with a picture of a dry court. Doesn't matter. They'll ask.

In my day you looked out of the window. If it was raining you didn't go. If it wasn't raining you went, and if it started raining when you got there you stood in the clubhouse and complained about it, which was half the fun. Nobody rang anybody. Nobody could.

Eight turned up. Lovely session. Didn't rain.

At 8.15, halfway through a drill, my phone buzzed in my bag. I knew. I didn't even look. I knew.

"Is it still on?"
