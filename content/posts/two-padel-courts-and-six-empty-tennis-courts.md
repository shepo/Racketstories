We have two padel courts and six tennis courts. The two padel courts are full from seven in the morning until ten at night. On a Tuesday afternoon in November you can stand on tennis court five and hear yourself think.

Every club with padel has this. I have now been asked about it at the bar, in the car park, by email and once in the gents. These are the questions, and the answers I gave.

![Padel doubles](just-friendly "Court two at 6.15 on a Wednesday. Booked since the previous Wednesday.")

<!--more-->

**Can we build another padel court?**

Yes. It costs about the same as a small house in the north and slightly more than a large shed in the south. We have the money for the shed.

**Can we turn a tennis court into a padel court?**

Yes. You can get two padel courts on one tennis court, roughly. I raised this. The tennis section raised several things back, most of them at volume.

**Why can't I get a padel booking?**

Because the booking opens at seven in the morning a week ahead, and about forty people are awake at 6.59 with their thumb over the button. I have tried it. I got 21.40 on a Thursday, which is after my bedtime.

**Why are the tennis courts empty?**

They aren't empty. They're quiet. There is a difference, and the tennis section would like it noted.

**So how full are they?**

I counted, one week in October, with a clipboard, from the clubhouse window. It was an odd week for me. The two padel courts were booked for 204 hours out of a possible 210. The six tennis courts were booked for 262 hours out of a possible 630. I have checked those figures twice and I still don't like them.

**Then why not convert one tennis court?**

See above. At volume.

**What does the committee think?**

The committee thinks it needs more information. The committee has thought that since March.

**Is it the same people on the padel courts every time?**

Mostly, yes. Four people book a court, the same four, at the same time, every week. They're perfectly nice and very quick on their thumbs. Meanwhile another sixty members have tried padel once, liked it, and never got near a court again.

**Is that fair?**

I'm the chair. I'm not allowed to have opinions about fair. I'm allowed to have opinions about the biscuits, and even that's contested.

**So what's the answer?**

Two padel courts, six tennis courts, and the same question every week. I've said it in the car park and I'll say it at the AGM. We don't have too few courts. We have two that everyone wants and six that some people want, and nobody wants to be the one who says it out loud.

Except me, apparently. In the gents.
