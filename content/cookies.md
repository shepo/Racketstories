We'd love to say there's a tin of biscuits involved. There isn't. This page lists what the blog stores in your browser, and when.

## If you say no, or don't choose

We store one thing: your answer, so we don't keep asking. Visits are still counted, but without cookies. PostHog works out a short-lived anonymous code on its servers, nothing goes onto your device, and we can't recognise you from one day to the next. More about that on the [privacy page](/p/privacy).

| Name | Where | What it's for | How long |
|---|---|---|---|
| `rs_cookies` | Browser storage | Remembers whether you said yes or no to cookies | Until you clear it |

## If you say yes

PostHog also stores an anonymous ID, so we can tell when someone comes back and which stories they read on different days. It doesn't contain your name or anything else about you, and it's never shared with advertisers.

| Name | Where | What it's for | How long |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ph_…_posthog` | Cookie and browser storage | An anonymous ID for PostHog analytics, so return visits are counted as one person | 1 year |
| `rs_cookies` | Browser storage | Remembers that you said yes | Until you clear it |

Your choice takes effect from the next page you open.

## No other cookies

There are no advertising cookies, no social media buttons that track you, no embedded videos and no fonts from other sites. Links to Facebook and Rallio only set their cookies once you're on their sites.

## Changing your mind

Use the button below to bring the cookie question back. If you change from yes to no, we delete the PostHog ID straight away. You can also clear cookies and site data for racketstories.com in your browser settings.
