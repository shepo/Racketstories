Hello! Racket Stories is a little group blog written by people who play at (and, in some cases, organise far too much at) a big multi-sport club in Surrey. Tennis mostly, but also padel, squash, pickleball, and the occasional heated table tennis rally in the bar.

We write about the bits of racket sports nobody puts in the brochure: the WhatsApp group with 140 members and 2 talkers, the 6.58pm cancellation, the ladder nobody updates, the beginner on court two who just wants a rally that lasts longer than a sneeze.

## The honest bit

We'd rather you knew this up front.

- **Jake is real.** A member at our club, Jake built [Rallio](https://rallio.io), the app our socials, ladders and mixers run on. Several posts here mention it, because it really is what we use.
- **Everyone else is a composite.** That's Bev, Dan, Priya, Graham, and the coaches and club chair who write here too (Coach Terry, Coach Mel and Malcolm). Each one is stitched together from real people and real things that happened at real clubs (we've all met a Keith). Names and details are changed so nobody's mum recognises them, and so we can be rude about the booking system without getting a letter from the committee.
- **The stories started life as articles on the Rallio blog.** We've retold them the way they actually felt from the court, the clubhouse and the car park.

## Counting visitors

We count visits with [PostHog](https://posthog.com), hosted in the EU, so we can see which stories people read and which links they click. It runs without cookies and doesn't store anything in your browser, which is why there's no cookie banner. There's no tracking across other sites, no ads and no recordings of what you do on the page.

## Getting around

There isn't really a menu, sorry. It's that kind of blog. Use the *Blog Archive* and *Labels* on the right, the "Older Posts" link at the bottom of the page, or the search box at the very top. Feeling brave? Click "Next Blog»" and see where you end up.

## Get in touch

The conversation carries on in our **[Facebook group](https://www.facebook.com/groups/2578495509243593/)**. Tell us your own stories from the court: the gripes, the niggles, the triumphs, the 6.58pm cancellations. Tennis, padel, pickleball, squash, whatever you play. The one rule is be kind. Moan about the situation, never the person.

Spotted a typo (Graham will want to know) or want a private word? Use the [Rallio contact page](https://rallio.io/contact/). It goes to Jake. Or find us on court. We're the ones apologising.
